# Support

- **Platform status:** [status.phaemos.com](https://status.phaemos.com), checked first during an outage
- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/phaemos/phaemos/discussions) on the monorepo
- **Bugs:** [issues on phaemos/phaemos](https://github.com/phaemos/phaemos/issues/new/choose)
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue
- **General enquiries:** contact@phaemos.com or [phaemos.com/contact](https://phaemos.com/contact)
- **User support:** support@phaemos.com

The documentation lives at [docs.phaemos.com](https://docs.phaemos.com).
