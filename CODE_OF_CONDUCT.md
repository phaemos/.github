# Code of Conduct

## Standards

PHAEMOS welcomes engineers, students and makers at every level. Anyone taking part in any PHAEMOS repository, discussion or issue is expected to be respectful, give specific and constructive feedback and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, abuse or personal attacks of any kind. Spam or off-topic promotion. Sharing anyone's private information without consent. Posting real operational data from someone else's equipment without permission.

## Enforcement

> [!NOTE]
> Report unacceptable behaviour to contact@phaemos.com or through [phaemos.com/contact](https://phaemos.com/contact). Reports are reviewed and appropriate action is taken, including removing content or blocking users where necessary.
